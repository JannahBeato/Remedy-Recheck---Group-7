Problem and users

“Fix made” does not always mean “issue solved.”

A corrective action can be completed while its effect on workers remains unresolved or unclear. Remedy Recheck keeps those two facts separate so the next decision is not lost.

How it works

One clear path from action to outcome.

Remedy Recheck adds a visible outcome checkpoint to supplier follow-up without replacing audits, worker feedback channels, or human judgement.

Step 01 · Record the fix

Implementation stays visible, but it is not treated as the final result.

Remedy Recheck records the issue category, agreed action, responsible owner, completion date, and supporting evidence for each supplier case.

✓ Demonstrated in the working application

A focused reviewer workspace

  • Protected sign-in and a session-based reviewer identity
  • Due-date ordering, search, filters, and outcome rechecks
  • Next actions, owners, due dates, and dated audit history
  • Persistent case updates and object-oriented components

→ Required for IKEA deployment

Organization-level protection

  • IKEA identity integration and server-side role permissions
  • Encrypted transfer and storage in a protected backend
  • Separated worker identities with controlled retention and deletion
  • Central access logs, backup, recovery, and governance

User-centered development

Research, prototype, test, and improve.

The design-thinking process is non-linear. New evidence can move the team back to an earlier stage whenever assumptions need to be reconsidered.

01

Empathize

Understand people and context

Study reviewer decisions, supplier responsibilities, and safe worker participation.
02

Define

Separate fix from outcome

Frame follow-up clarity as the core decision problem.
03

Ideate

Compare possible responses

Choose the smallest useful feature without replacing existing channels.
04

Prototype

Make the recheck tangible

Connect the action, evidence, outcome, owner, and next due date.
05

Test

Observe and revise

Check understanding, usefulness, privacy, and unintended effects.

Research and feedback

Each test must inform a decision.

The team records whose experience was studied, what assumption was tested, what was observed, and whether the result means continue, revise, investigate further, or stop.

Sustainability and IKEA value

Social value first, with the full footprint in view.

The Sustainability Awareness Framework helps the team examine individual, social, economic, environmental, and technical effects together.

Social

Support fair treatment and worker voice

Unresolved outcomes remain visible, while feedback routes are designed to reduce retaliation risk.

Individual

Protect agency, safety, and privacy

Workers can contribute through protected routes without their identity appearing in the operational case overview.

Economic

Focus resources on unresolved cases

Clear ownership and due dates help teams direct effort where follow-up is still needed.

Environmental

Limit the ICT footprint

The service is designed for existing devices, restrained storage, and defined evidence-retention periods.

Technical

Build for accountable use

A deployment needs secure access, reliable data, maintainable software, backups, and lifecycle controls.

Orders of effects

From the software itself to wider change.

Each level includes benefits, resource use, and possible unintended consequences.

First order

Immediate effects

Building, hosting, storing, using, and eventually retiring Remedy Recheck require energy, equipment, maintenance, and staff time.

Second order

Enabling effects

Maria can identify unresolved outcomes sooner, assign ownership, and follow up before a case disappears from view.

Third order

Structural effects

Repeated use can change expectations about supplier accountability, worker participation, and what counts as a completed remedy.

Measuring improvement

From supplier risk to measurable social impact.

Challenge 4 asks IKEA to identify high-risk suppliers, monitor worker conditions, and measure social-impact improvements. Remedy Recheck connects those aims to practical follow-up decisions.

  1. 01
    Monitor the process

    Track overdue actions, completed fixes, outcome checks, repeat issues, and time to follow-up.

  2. 02
    Monitor the human outcome

    Record whether conditions improved and whether the feedback route was understood, accessible, and safe.

  3. 03
    Protect the people behind the data

    Minimize personal data, restrict access, log changes, apply retention rules, and escalate retaliation concerns through qualified channels.
