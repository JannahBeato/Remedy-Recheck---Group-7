Protected by design

How this workspace protects people

Remedy Recheck limits what appears in the operational view and keeps every change accountable.

01

Pseudonymous cases

Operational screens use case IDs. Worker names do not appear in the case overview.

02

Separated identities

Identity information is kept outside case evidence and is available only through qualified protected channels.

03

Restricted access

Assigned roles control access to supplier follow-up information and outcome decisions.

04

Accountable changes

Outcome updates record the reviewer, date, decision, owner, and next due date in the audit history.

05

Protected lifecycle

Information is encrypted during transfer and storage, retained only for defined periods, and deleted when no longer required.
